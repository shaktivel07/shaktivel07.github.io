Principal Component Analysis for Dimensionality Reduction

PCA projects high-dimensional data onto the directions of greatest variance. The practical question is how many components to keep: enough to retain information, few enough to keep models simple and avoid overfitting.

Focus areas

Ongoing · 2026
  • Applying Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to reduce the dimensionality of high-dimensional feature sets while preserving maximum variance for downstream model training.
  • Evaluating explained-variance thresholds and eigenvalue decomposition to balance information retention against model complexity and overfitting risk.
